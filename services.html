Services

Practical dirt work and land cleanup services.

Land management and compact excavation services for rural homes, small acreage, farm ground, and access areas that need practical improvement, across Clay, Richland, Effingham, Jasper, Crawford, Coles, and Fayette Counties in Illinois.

Mini Excavator Work

Small trenching, drainage corrections, culvert prep, grading, stump-area cleanup, and tight-access excavation for projects that do not require full-size equipment.

Mowing

Pasture edges, lots, trails, and seasonal overgrowth control for properties that need more than lawn care.

Brush Clearing

Clearing saplings, heavy weeds, and tangled growth around access lanes, fields, trails, and rough edges to restore working space.

Fence Row Cleanup

Cutting back overgrowth and opening usable space along existing or planned fence lines for easier access and maintenance.

Driveway Leveling

Grading ruts, shaping gravel, filling low spots, and improving drainage so rural driveways are smoother and more reliable.

Deer Food Plots

Clearing brush, breaking and leveling ground, and seedbed prep to establish new deer food plots, plus seasonal mowing and refresh work to keep existing plots productive.

Garden Tilling

Breaking new ground and working up existing plots for gardens, raised beds, and planting areas — turning over sod and loosening packed soil so it is ready to plant.

Property Cleanup

Custom cleanup work for neglected corners, trails, building sites, field entrances, and small acreage projects.

Storm Damage Cleanup

Clearing downed trees, broken limbs, and scattered debris after storms and high wind — opening blocked driveways and access lanes, piling or hauling off fallen timber, and getting the property usable again.

Demolition & Teardown

Taking down old sheds, milk houses, and small outbuildings, pulling out the old foundation, and hauling off the debris so the site is cleared and ready for whatever comes next.

Trailer Hauling

Hauling tractors, equipment, scrap, and job debris with car and utility trailers — clearing off what a job leaves behind or moving your gear from one place to the next.

Simple project flow

  1. Walk the property. Review the access, slope, soil, drainage, and cleanup goals.
  2. Set the scope. Decide what gets cleared, moved, leveled, hauled, or left in place before the work begins.
  3. Get it usable. Complete the work with compact equipment sized for the job.
